Time in default and unclaimed money

County rules require taxes to go unpaid for at least 3 consecutive fiscal years before a parcel can be sold (Treasurer FAQ). After that, title passes to the County Treasurer as trustee, and that date is on the Assessor's ownership record. Estimated years unpaid = time held in trust + the 3 required years.
