    About this data

    A scheduled job checks the Clark County Treasurer auction site every 3 hours and republishes this page when anything changes. It reads the same data the county page loads, for every auction the county has on record. Parcel numbers link to the Assessor's parcel detail. Older printable results are on the county's auction information page.

    "No street address" means the county lists the location as unassigned, which usually indicates vacant land. Minimum bid is the opening bid set by the county (taxes, penalties and costs). Excess proceeds are as reported by the county. This dashboard is informational and is not affiliated with Clark County; confirm details with the Treasurer's office before relying on them.
